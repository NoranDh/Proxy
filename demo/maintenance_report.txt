MAINTENANCE DEPARTMENT - FAILURE REPORT (SAMPLE DATA FOR DEMONSTRATION)

Equipment: Crude charge pump P-101A, single-stage overhung centrifugal pump (API 610 OH2)
Driver: 250 kW electric motor, 2980 rpm
Bearings: drive-end deep-groove ball bearing 6314, non-drive-end angular contact pair 7314, oil-bath lubricated (ISO VG 68 mineral oil)

Event: Pump tripped on high bearing temperature and seized on 14 March at 02:40. Standby pump P-101B was started at 02:55.

Findings on teardown (14-15 March):
- Drive-end bearing inner race shows blue/brown heat discoloration and smeared raceway surface.
- Bearing cage fractured in two places; several balls flattened.
- Bearing housing oil was dark, with a burnt smell. Level was below the bottom of the sight glass.
- Oil sample contained visible metallic particles.
- Mechanical seal faces show scoring and heat checking. Seal failure appears secondary to shaft movement after the bearing collapsed.
- Shaft journal under the drive-end bearing is scored; shaft runout measured 0.12 mm (limit 0.05 mm).
- No evidence of water ingress or process fluid in the bearing housing.

Maintenance history:
- Last bearing replacement: 26 months ago (planned overhaul).
- Last recorded oil change: 9 months ago. The preventive maintenance task for a 3-month oil change was deferred three times because of a work-order backlog.
- The constant-level oiler bottle was found empty. It is not on the operator daily round checklist.
- No previous bearing failures recorded on P-101A in the last 5 years.

Repair: bearings, mechanical seal and shaft replaced. Pump returned to standby on 18 March.
